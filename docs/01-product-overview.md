# Product Overview

## Problem

Contractors complete valuable projects but often fail to preserve the evidence of that work in a structured, reusable form.

Project evidence typically becomes fragmented across:

- WhatsApp conversations
- Email inboxes
- Google Drive / OneDrive
- Employee laptops
- Phones
- Paper folders
- Company-profile documents
- Tender folders

When the contractor later needs to prove previous experience for a tender, RFQ, proposal, prospective client, financier, or partner, the business has to reconstruct its own history.

## Product thesis

Completed work should accumulate into a reusable company asset.

Contractor Experience Vault organizes project history around the project itself instead of around folders and files.

```text
Project
|
+-- Client and project facts
+-- Scope
+-- Dates and value
+-- Appointment / contract evidence
+-- Completion evidence
+-- Client references
+-- Before / progress / after photographs
+-- Other supporting material
```

## Core positioning

Do not position the product as cloud storage.

Preferred positioning:

> Turn every completed job into evidence that can help win the next one.

Alternative:

> Never lose proof of work your company has already completed.

## Target customer

Initial focus:

- Owner-managed contractors
- 5-50 employees
- 10+ completed projects
- Project-based revenue
- Recurring need to demonstrate previous experience
- Informal or fragmented project-document management
- Decision-maker reachable directly

Initial verticals:

1. Electrical
2. Solar
3. Construction subcontracting
4. CCTV/security
5. ICT/networking
6. Facilities maintenance
7. Plumbing/mechanical

## What customers are buying

They are not primarily buying:

- Storage
- A project-management platform
- A CRM
- Tender discovery
- Job cards

They are buying:

- Faster preparation of company profiles and tenders
- Better presentation of previous experience
- Reduced risk of losing important evidence
- A permanent institutional memory of completed work
- A professional reusable project portfolio

## V1 economic hypothesis

A service-led pilot can be sold before SaaS pricing is finalized.

Possible initial offer:

> Give us five of your completed projects. We will organize the evidence and turn them into a professional reusable project-experience portfolio.

Pricing is deliberately not locked in this version. The first goal is to prove willingness to provide real data, use the resulting portfolio, and pay for the outcome.
