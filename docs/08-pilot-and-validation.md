# Pilot and Validation Plan

## Goal

Validate that contractors value structured project history enough to use the system and eventually pay for it.

## First offer

Preferred pilot proposition:

> Give us five of your completed projects. We will organize the supporting evidence and turn them into a professional reusable project-experience portfolio.

This is service-led on purpose.

The founder helps load the initial project history in order to observe the actual workflow and document mess.

## Customer profile

Prioritize:

- Owner-managed contractor
- 5-50 employees
- At least 10 completed projects
- Regular RFQs/tenders/proposals
- Owner directly reachable
- Existing project evidence is fragmented
- Business has reason to look more professional when presenting previous work

## What to collect from the first customer

For each project:

- Project name
- Client
- Location
- Approximate value
- Dates
- Scope
- Description
- Appointment letter / contract / PO
- Completion certificate
- Reference
- Project photos
- Any evidence they normally attach to proposals/tenders

## Observe the source channels

Do not force the customer into the ideal workflow during research.

Record where their evidence actually comes from:

- WhatsApp
- Email
- Google Drive
- OneDrive
- Phone gallery
- USB
- Physical folders
- Old company profile
- Tender folders

This becomes roadmap evidence.

## Validation hierarchy

### Weak
"They like the idea."

### Better
They allow real project data into the pilot.

### Strong
They use a generated project profile or experience pack in real work.

### Stronger
They return to add additional projects.

### Very strong
They refer another contractor.

### Commercial validation
They pay.

## Three-pilot goal

Pilot 1:
- Learn ingestion mess
- Learn terminology
- Learn what evidence matters most

Pilot 2:
- Determine whether the same problems repeat
- Refine completeness logic
- Refine PDF presentation

Pilot 3:
- Test whether onboarding can become repeatable
- Test willingness to pay
- Test referral potential

## Questions to answer before expanding V1

- Which evidence is requested most frequently?
- How often are completion certificates missing?
- Are appointment letters or purchase orders more common?
- Do contractors care about storing contract value?
- Do contractors want private internal records and separate client-facing records?
- Are photos useful in tenders or primarily sales/company profiles?
- Who actually assembles tender evidence today?
- How long does evidence preparation currently take?
- Do customers already maintain a project schedule spreadsheet?
- What would make them return after initial setup?

## Pilot success condition

Do not use signups as the main metric.

The first meaningful success threshold is:

> At least three contractors entrust real project evidence to the system, use the output, and at least one pays or demonstrates clear willingness to pay.
